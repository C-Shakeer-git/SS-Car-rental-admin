How to Connect Your Own Live Google Sheet (Optional):
Step 1: Create Google Sheet

Go to sheets.new and create a blank spreadsheet named "Lakshmi Narayana Travels Data". Copy the Spreadsheet ID from the URL.

Step 2: Get Service Account Key

In Google Cloud Console, enable Google Sheets API & Google Drive API. Create a Service Account and download its JSON key as credentials.json.

Step 3: Share & Update Properties

Click Share on the Google Sheet and give "Editor" permission to the service account email. In application.properties set google.sheets.enabled=true and paste your spreadsheetId!
